  "summary": "This study investigates alginate polylysine microcapsules as immune barriers for islet transplantation, comparing poly-L-lysine (PLL) and poly-D-lysine (PDL) membranes. It measures permeability to cytokines (TNF, IL-1β) and immunoglobulins (IgG). All capsules were impermeable to IgG; PLL-membranes (but not PDL) allowed transferrin passage; IL-1β penetrated all capsules; high-G capsules could exclude TNF. The study provides evidence that PDL-based membranes differ in permeability from PLL, relevant to immune barrier function, but does not directly assess adaptive immune outcomes or immunotherapy.",
